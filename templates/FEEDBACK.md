# Friction with the ritual

Agents write here when the wording of a skill or of the watchdog got in the way, led past the mark or
did not cover the case. This is worked through by the `kit-review` skill: it reads the entries, the
watchdog's events and the session journals, and proposes pointed edits of the wordings to the author.

Write briefly and factually, without proposals of "how it should be" — the proposal is produced at
the review, when the repeatability is visible. One situation — one entry, not to be edited after the
fact.

Format:

```
## <ISO time> · <skill or hook> · <project>
- SAID: <verbatim, the part of the instruction that worked out wrong>
- CAME OUT: <what I did or did not do as a result>
- WHAT WAS MISSING: <which distinction, condition, example>
```
